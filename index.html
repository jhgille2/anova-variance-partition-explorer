Estimator behavior over repeated trials

Distribution of the entry-mean ĥ² estimate across Monte Carlo repetitions of the whole trial. The blue line is the true h²; the orange dashed line is the MC mean. Narrower, well-centered distributions mean ANOVA partitions the variance reliably under that design.

Statistical methodology

Model

Balanced g × e × r trial, RCBD within environments (replicates nested in environments). All effects random and independent:

yijk = μ + Gi + Ej + GEij + R(E)jk + εijk

Gi ~ N(0, σ²_G), Ej ~ N(0, σ²_E), GEij ~ N(0, σ²_GE), R(E)jk ~ N(0, σ²_R), εijk ~ N(0, σ²_ε).

Variance component estimators

Method of moments, equating observed MS to E[MS], e.g. σ̂²_G = (MS_G − MS_GE)/(r·e), σ̂²_GE = (MS_GE − MS_error)/r. Negative estimates are truncated at zero for heritability (standard practice; the raw values are shown in the ANOVA table).

Heritability

h² = σ²_G / (σ²_G + σ²_GE/e + σ²_ε/(r·e))

Entry-mean (line-mean) basis: the squared correlation between the estimated line mean and the true genotypic value, and the relevant quantity for response to selection among lines (Holland, Nyquist & Cervantes-Martinez, 2003).

Assumptions and limitations

  • Perfectly balanced data; no missing plots.
  • All effects random, independent, and Gaussian; no spatial trend or heteroscedasticity within environments.
  • Method-of-moments estimation (not REML); F-tests assume normality.
  • Rep(environment) variance is design noise; it does not enter the entry-mean heritability, which conditions on the trial as run.
  • With r = 1, genotype × environment and residual variance cannot be separated; the app pools them, as is conventional for unreplicated trials.
